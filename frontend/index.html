📋 How to use the device ▾
1
Wear the device properly

Strap the sensor securely to your fingertip or wrist as instructed. Ensure the optical HR sensor and GSR electrodes have firm, consistent skin contact before powering on.

2
Power on — listen for 2 beeps

When the device boots it plays 2 short buzzer beeps. This confirms firmware has initialised and Bluetooth is broadcasting. Wait for the status bar above to turn green before starting a session.

3
One buzz = one data point recorded

After connecting, the buzzer pulses once per second. Each buzz means HR and GSR values have been sampled and sent to this dashboard. The live chart updates in sync.

⚠
Keep your hand still — no accelerometer on board

This device does not include an accelerometer, so motion artefacts cannot be filtered automatically. Any movement of the hand or wrist while wearing the device will cause false HR spikes that the ML model will misclassify as stress or anxiety.

✅ Correct use: wear the device, place both hands on a flat surface, and sit calmly for the duration of the session.

4
Calibration window (~30 s)

The first 30 seconds are used to establish your personal baseline HR and GSR. Sit still during this period. The footer will change from "Baseline calibrating…" to show your baseline values once complete.
